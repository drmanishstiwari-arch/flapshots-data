# FlapShots

Head and neck evidence in three minutes a day — swipe-card summaries of new papers, guidelines and news in head and neck oncology and microvascular reconstruction, by Dr Manish S. Tiwari.

Live at **https://flapshots.drmanishtiwari.com**

- `index.html` — the app (no sign-in; likes, saves and streaks stay on the reader's device)
- `cards.json` — the card library, refreshed automatically by the FlapShots paper and news pipeline
